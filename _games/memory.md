## How to play

Sixteen cards, eight matching pairs, all face down. Flip two at a time; if they match
they stay up, if they don't they turn back over.

- Clear the board in as few moves as possible.
- **Best** records your lowest move count, so lower is better here.
- Pairs left is your progress: 8 down, 0 to go.

### How to actually remember

Random guessing needs roughly **27 moves** on average. Paying attention gets you
under **20**.

- Say the position out loud as you flip — "top-left is a rocket". Naming it doubles recall.
- Flip a card you already know about first if you're unsure about its partner.
- Early on, deliberately reveal new cards; information is worth more than a lucky match.
