## How to play

Pieces fall one at a time. Move and rotate them to pack the board as tightly as you
can — every completed horizontal line disappears and everything above it drops down.

- Clear four lines at once with an I-piece for a **Tetris**: 1200 points at level 1.
- The game speeds up every ten lines.
- The stack reaching the top ends the run.

### Scoring

| Lines cleared | Points |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 100 × level |
| 2 | 300 × level |
| 3 | 500 × level |
| 4 | 800 × level |

Soft-dropping adds 1 point per cell, hard-dropping adds 2.

### Tips

- Keep the leftmost or rightmost column open for I-pieces and only cash it in on a Tetris.
- Don't clear a single line when you're two pieces away from a quad — unless you're in danger.
- Flat is fast. Every hole you leave behind is a hole you have to dig out later.
