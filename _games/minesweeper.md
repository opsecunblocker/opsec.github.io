## How to play

Reveal every square that isn't a mine. Each number tells you how many of the eight
neighbouring squares are mines.

- Left click reveals a square.
- Right click (or long-press on touch) plants a flag where you think a mine is.
- Clicking a numbered square that already has all its mines flagged reveals its
  remaining neighbours — a huge time saver.
- Your first click is always safe, and always opens an area.

### Difficulty

This board is **9 × 9 with 10 mines** — the classic Beginner grid, tuned to be
winnable in about a minute once you know the patterns.

### Patterns worth memorising

- **1-2-1** along a flat edge: the mines are under the two 1s.
- **1-1** against a wall: the mine is the square beyond the second 1.
- A corner **1** with only one hidden neighbour: that neighbour is a mine.

### Scoring

There's no score — your best is your fastest clear time.
