## How to play

Click the panel, wait for it to turn green, then click as fast as you can.

- Five attempts per round, averaged at the end.
- Click **before** it turns green and that attempt is void — no second chances.
- **Best** is your fastest single reaction, so lower is better.

### What's a good number?

- **Under 200 ms** — genuinely quick, especially on a phone.
- **200–250 ms** — around typical human average for a visual reaction.
- **250–300 ms** — normal for a laptop trackpad.
- **Over 300 ms** — usually a display with high input lag, or a slow refresh rate.

If your times look suspiciously slow, try a different browser — display latency
accounts for more of the number than most people expect.
