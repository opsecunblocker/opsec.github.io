## How to play

The four pads light up in a sequence. Repeat it back exactly. Get it right and the
sequence grows by one. Get it wrong and the game ends.

- Each round replays the whole sequence from the beginning before your turn.
- The sequence gets faster at round 5 and again at round 9.
- **Round** is how long your current sequence is.

### Remembering longer sequences

Most people stall around **7 items** — that's roughly the capacity of short-term
memory. Getting past it takes a trick rather than effort.

- **Chunk it.** 4-2-3-1 becomes "forty-two, thirty-one". Three chunks beat four digits.
- **Say the colours** under your breath as they play. Audio + visual beats visual alone.
- **Associate, don't rehearse.** Red-red-blue is "two bricks and the sky".
- Watch the whole sequence without committing to an answer until it finishes.
