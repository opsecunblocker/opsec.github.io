## How to play

Clear all five rows of invaders before they reach your bunkers. Shoot through your own
bunker walls if you have to — they take damage from both sides.

- You can only have **one shot in flight** at a time, so make it count.
- Invaders speed up as the row thins out.
- Clear the fleet and the next wave starts faster, with the rows one step lower.

### Scoring

- Rear rows: **30 points**
- Middle rows: **20 points**
- Front rows: **10 points**
- Clearing a wave awards a **200 point** bonus. Bunkers stay damaged between waves.

### Tips

- Clear one vertical column early: it gives you a firing lane all the way to the back rows.
- The bottom corners are the safest place to sit.
- Don't spam the trigger. A wasted shot is a second where you can't defend yourself.
