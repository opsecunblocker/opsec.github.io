## How to play

Every move slides all tiles on the board in the direction you choose. Two tiles with
the same number merge into one tile with double the value, and a new tile spawns.

- A move only counts if something actually shifts.
- Each merge adds the new tile's value to your score.
- The board is full and no merges are possible — that's game over.

### Strategy

The single most useful habit in 2048 is **picking a corner and keeping your biggest
tile there**. Build a descending snake of values along one edge and never slide in
the direction that would pull your anchor out of its corner.

- Keep the bottom row ordered from largest to smallest.
- Avoid sliding up when your anchor is bottom-left.
- Merging small pairs promptly is safer than hoarding them.

### Tiles

2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128, 256, 512, 1024, 2048 — and if you're very good, well past it.
