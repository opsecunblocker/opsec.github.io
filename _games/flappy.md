## How to play

Tap to flap upward. Gravity does the rest. Thread the gaps between pipes without
touching one or hitting the ground.

- One point per pipe you get past.
- There's no ceiling, but flying too high makes the next gap much harder to enter.
- The first flap starts the game.

### Getting past ten points

- **Flap early, flap small.** Panic-flapping to the top of the gap leaves you with
  no room to correct.
- Aim to enter each gap slightly low and rising, then let gravity settle you into it.
- Watch the second pipe, not the one you're passing. Your next input is for the next gap.
- Keep a rhythm. Most runs die from one over-correction, not from a hard gap.
