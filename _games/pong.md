## How to play

Slide your paddle up and down and return the ball. Miss it and your opponent scores.

- First to **11 points** wins the match.
- The ball speeds up with every hit, up to a limit.
- Where it strikes your paddle determines the return angle — the edges send it
  back much sharper than the centre.

### Beating the computer

The AI tracks the ball, but not perfectly: it moves at a fixed top speed and only
reacts once the ball crosses the halfway line.

- Aim for the extremes. Big angles force it to cross the whole screen in one rally.
- Serve into a corner so its first move is already a long one.
- It gets slightly less accurate as the ball speeds up, so long rallies favour you.
