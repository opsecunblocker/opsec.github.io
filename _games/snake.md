## How to play

Steer the snake around the board and eat the glowing pellets. Every pellet makes you
one segment longer — and one step closer to trapping yourself.

- Hit a wall or your own tail and the run is over.
- You can't reverse direction; turns have to be made one axis at a time.
- The snake speeds up slightly every five pellets.

### Scoring

Each pellet is worth **10 points**, plus a small bonus that grows as your snake gets
longer. A clean run at high speed is worth far more than a cautious one.

### Tips

- Keep looping near the edges early on; the open middle is where you'll need room later.
- Don't chase a pellet into a corner unless you can already see your way back out.
- After about 30 pellets, start thinking two moves ahead instead of one.
