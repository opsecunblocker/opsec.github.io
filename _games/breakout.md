## How to play

Bounce the ball off your paddle and destroy the brick wall above you. Clear every
brick to advance to the next level, which brings a faster ball and a denser wall.

- You get **three lives**. Miss the ball and you lose one.
- Where the ball hits the paddle changes the bounce angle — use it to aim.
- Bricks near the top of the wall are worth more points.

### Scoring

- Top rows: **7 points** each
- Upper middle: **5 points**
- Lower middle: **3 points**
- Bottom rows: **1 point**
- Clearing a level awards a **100 point** bonus.

### Tips

- Hit the ball with the edges of the paddle for sharp sideways angles.
- A steep vertical angle is slow and safe; shallow angles clear rows faster.
- Once you've tunnelled a hole up one side, you can keep ricocheting inside it for huge points.
